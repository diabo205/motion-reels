# Project: Pilot Officer Rashid Minhas (NH) 1971 Reel

## Overview
A high-retention 9:16 kinetic motion graphics documentary reel detailing the heroic sacrifice of Pilot Officer Rashid Minhas on 20 August 1971, when his T-33 jet trainer was hijacked by Flight Lieutenant Matiur Rahman heading towards India, leading Minhas to force the aircraft into the ground 32 miles from the border.

## Specifications
- **Resolution**: 1080 x 1920 (9:16 vertical)
- **Framerate**: 60 FPS
- **Audio**: `assets/audio/bgm.mp3`

## Directory Structure
- `plates/`: Historical photographs and archival portraits (`rashid_minhas.jpg`, `rashid_minhas_hd.jpg`).
- `audit/`: 18 rendered audit still frames.
- `final_reel.mp4`: Rendered production video.
