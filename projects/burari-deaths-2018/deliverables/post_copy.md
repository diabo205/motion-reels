Historical Sources & Fact-Check Citations:
• Eleven members of the Chundawat family found dead in their Burari residence on 1 July 2018 — Source: Delhi Police Charge Sheet / The Hindu (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• The dead included grandmother Narayani Devi (80), her sons, daughters, and two 15-year-old grandsons — Source: Delhi Police FSL Autopsy Report (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• Priyanka's engagement was celebrated with 200 guests just 14 days before the tragedy — Source: The Hindu / Indian Express (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• The death of patriarch Bhopal Singh in 2007 plunged his youngest son Lalit into profound grief — Source: Delhi Police Investigation Dossier (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• Forensic handwriting analysis confirmed Priyanka and others wrote down Lalit's dictated trances — Source: Delhi Police Forensic Handwriting Division (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• The diaries detailed 'Badh Tapasya' (Banyan Tree worship) where members would imitate hanging aerial roots — Source: Delhi Police Investigation Records (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• The final diary entry assured: 'The earth will shake, the sky will part, and I will appear to untie everyone' — Source: Delhi Police Burari Diary Excerpt (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• Eighty-year-old grandmother Narayani Devi was found strangled in her bed, unable to stand on a stool — Source: Delhi Police Post-Mortem Report (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• Tommy, the family's Indian pariah dog, was found chained on the rooftop terrace barking continuously with high fever — Source: Animal Rights / Delhi Police Records (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
• Medical experts concluded the deaths were caused by 'shared psychosis' (folie à plusieurs) without suicidal intent — Source: Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) / AIIMS Delhi (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burari_deaths)
