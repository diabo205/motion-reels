# The Burari Deaths (2018) — Motion Graphics Documentary Reel

## Overview
A haunting, deeply emotional, beat-synced 9:16 historical motion graphics documentary investigating the tragic 2018 Burari deaths in Sant Nagar, Delhi, India. On 1 July 2018, eleven members spanning three generations of the Chundawat (Bhatia) family were found dead inside their two-story home—ten hanging from an iron ceiling mesh resembling the aerial hanging roots of a banyan tree (*Badh Tapasya*), and the eighty-year-old grandmother in an adjacent room.

Recovered handwritten registers spanning eleven years revealed a collective shared delusion (*folie à plusieurs*) led by son Lalit, who believed he was possessed by his deceased father Bhopal Singh. The family believed the ritual was an act of divine penance that would lead to supernatural salvation, not death.
